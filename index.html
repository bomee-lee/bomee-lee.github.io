About

Bomee Lee is a Seoul-based English copywriter with a background in English interpretation and translation at both undergraduate and graduate levels.

Starting her career in localization, she has since worked across both the Korean localization of English-language copy and the globalization of Korean creative work.

Her practice has expanded beyond translating language to transcreation — reinterpreting culture, context and emotion to make creative ideas resonate across markets.
